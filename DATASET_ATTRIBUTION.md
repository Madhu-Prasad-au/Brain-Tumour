# Dataset attribution

The training notebooks use the Labeled MRI Brain Tumor Dataset by Ali Rostami, published through [Roboflow Universe](https://universe.roboflow.com/ali-rostami/labeled-mri-brain-tumor-dataset) under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0).

Dataset image files are not included in this repository. Download the dataset from its source, preserve its license and attribution, and arrange it under `Tumour/train`, `Tumour/valid`, and `Tumour/test` before running the training notebooks.
